Our Practice

Independent eye care in Newburgh, led by Dr. Elaina M. Groo and Dr. John Kaknis, with licensed opticians and a full clinical team.

Dr. Elaina M. Groo

Dr. Groo graduated from the SUNY College of Optometry in 1993. She is licensed and certified to treat and manage ocular disease and glaucoma, and to diagnose visual conditions associated with diabetes, hypertension, and other systemic conditions.

She completed internships in contact lenses and vision therapy, including infant, toddler, pediatric, and learning-disabilities clinics. She fits specialty lenses, including multifocals for presbyopia and scleral lenses for keratoconus, corneal transplants, and corneal dystrophies.

She also holds an A.A.S. in Ophthalmic Dispensing from New York City Technical College and is a member of the New York State Optometric Association, Hudson Valley Optometric Society, American Optometric Association, and the Optometric Extension Program.

Dr. John Kaknis

Dr. Kaknis is a SUNY Optometry graduate and has provided optometry care in the Newburgh community since 1987. He is experienced in comprehensive examinations and in the diagnosis and treatment of a wide range of eye diseases and conditions.

He is certified in dry eye and corneal care, and in orthokeratology for nearsightedness. Both doctors maintain certification to prescribe and manage oral therapeutic agents, including antibiotics, antivirals, and antiglaucoma medications.

A lifelong Orange County resident, Dr. Kaknis is committed to helping patients keep clear, comfortable vision. Ask us about Upneeq and Vuity.
