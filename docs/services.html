Eye Care & Eyewear

Services & What’s New

State-of-the-art eye care and eyewear, from pediatric exams through medical treatment, specialty lenses, and glasses made with an on-site optical.

Exams

Comprehensive examinations from pediatrics to geriatrics, complex prescriptions, myopia control, and free consultations for laser vision correction and refractive surgery.

Medical Eye Care

Ocular disease, vitreous floaters, allergies, infection, cataract assessment and co-management, diabetic eye exams, glaucoma, low vision, dry eye and punctal plugs, and thyroid eye disease assessment.

Imaging

Fundus photography, optical coherence tomography, visual field testing, and biophotonic scanning for macular pigment.

Contact Lenses

Soft, astigmatic, multifocal, keratoconus, and scleral lens fitting and evaluation, with ongoing supervision.

Medical Coverage

Most Medical Plans

We participate with many medical insurances, including Medicare, UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross and Blue Shield, The Empire Plan, MVP, Cigna, CDPHP, Aetna, Wellcare, and Affinity. Ask the staff if you have another plan.

Medical coverage and vision coverage are not the same. Our staff can explain which applies to your visit.

Vision Plans

Glasses & Contacts

Vision plans accepted include VSP, EyeMed, Davis, Spectera, Affinity, and others. Many plans require us to use their laboratory. Our licensed opticians check every order against ANSI standards before it is dispensed.

We accept cash, CareCredit, and credit or debit cards (American Express, Mastercard, Visa, Discover). A card fee of 3.5% may apply. You will be told the total and any cash discount at the time of service.

The office is a stand-alone, wheelchair-accessible building. If you have questions about coverage, call 845-565-2020.
