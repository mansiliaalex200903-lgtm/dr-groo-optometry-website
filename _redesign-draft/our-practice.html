Dr. Elaina M. Groo

Dr. Groo graduated from SUNY College of Optometry in 1993. She is licensed and certified to treat and manage ocular disease and glaucoma, and to diagnose visual conditions associated with diabetes, hypertension, and many systemic conditions.

She completed internship work in contact lenses and vision therapy, including infant/toddler, pediatric, and learning-disabilities settings. She fits specialty contact lenses including multifocal and scleral designs.

Dr. Groo is a member of the NY Optometric Association, Hudson Valley Optometric Association, American Optometric Association, and Optometric Extension Program.

Dr. John Kaknis

Dr. Kaknis is a SUNY Optometry graduate and has served the Newburgh community since 1987. He specializes in comprehensive vision examinations and the diagnosis and treatment of a wide range of eye diseases and conditions.

He is certified in dry eye and corneal care, and in orthokeratology for myopia management. Our practice uses advanced diagnostic technology; ask us about Upneeq and Vuity.

Our team

Licensed opticians Cheryl and Dave, optical staff Anne, reception Vicky and Carolina, and optometric assistants Glenda, Vicky, and Yaritza support your care from exam through eyewear and contact lenses.
